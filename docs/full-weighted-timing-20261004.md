# Complete four-control qualified-input timing

This stage measures computation before any real weighted likelihood fitting.
It retains all 20,832,000 potential candidate identities across the original
4,340 cohorts, 130,200 designs and 260,400 response inputs. All four residual
controls, both loading modes, all five trees, both outcomes and ML/REML remain
in scope. Original source exclusions, constants and covariance reviews remain
in the census and are never converted into eligible timing groups.

## Selection and numerical measurements

Each eligible cohort/control/mode/tree/outcome/method group selects the maximum
tuple `(active columns, normalized design condition, candidate ID)` across
all original designs. Up to 694,400 groups can be measured. Every census row
binds its candidate identity and, where eligible, its group's representative.
Equal background-node and background-pair diagonals retain separate policy
labels and separate groups.

At scaled variance points zero and one, the probe measures source validation,
primary constructor, fresh actual-D production qualification, reader's fresh
qualification plus independent latent products, independent component-spectral
constructor, and both likelihood evaluations. Residual D is never replaced by
uniform values. Named source certificates alone select q4/q5/q6 bases;
physical-pair exceptions remain present. Closed covariance envelopes and all
comparison tolerances stay unchanged.

The independent reader reconstructs the complete source census and every
maximum-tuple representative, replays **all** numerical probes, checks every
checkpoint/export and recomputes all planning sums. It verifies primary versus
independent likelihood agreement; it does not claim independent hardware
duration reproduction. Success or review cannot be fabricated by simply
rehashing a local artifact. Reusing a closed producer chunk also replays its
numeric probes before retaining its original bytes. Completed producer and
reader restarts, including alternate reader output paths, are refused.

Every construction or precision review retains the original rows, labels,
X, y, D and species factor. Content-addressed original arrays are shared across
review records, with original dtype, shape, raw-byte digest and archive hash.
Every referenced array is read and checked byte-for-byte. This removes repeated
matrix storage without removing any review or any species variation. Failed
and incomplete roots remain preserved; a new version is required for changes.

## Conditional fit planning

The unchanged three primary searches allow 1,503 evaluations including final
replays. Independent replay/curvature/search paths allow 1,522, 1,526 or 1,530
evaluations for q4, q5 or q6. Conditional planning multiplies the maximum
observed point duration by these explicit budgets and each group's original
eligible candidate count. It includes source validation, both production
qualification guards, independent qualification, and failed-search reproduction
versus successful-fit independent search paths.

These numbers do not bound unmeasured optimizer points, predict actual search
counts or establish convergence. Review groups remain unmeasured coverage.
Loading, export, closure and review-array I/O remain separate. No production
or project finish ETA follows from synthetic timings or this extrapolation.
Numerical agreement is not fit acceptance or inferential calibration.

## Software scope and preserved attempts

The checker uses all three declared five-cohort source grids: 72,000 candidate
identities, all controls/modes/trees/responses/methods, 320 eligible groups with
640 numerical points. Native probes are **not mocked**. Source closures and
original-journal fixtures are synthetic. A separate 64-group, 128-point check
covers common and exceptional pair bases and q4/q5/q6. These are software
qualification cases, not a fungal pilot or fitted biological effects.

The full grids check independently recomputed selection maxima, source and
numeric readback, conditional budget arithmetic, closed-chunk interruption and
byte-preserving continuation, rehashed checkpoint/export alterations, malformed
numeric claims and completed restart refusal. The array-custody fixture tests
sharing, original-byte replay and rejection of a modified stored array.

V1's checkpoint test assumed an eligible group in the first two cohorts;
both were exclusions, so it stopped with `StopIteration`. Its original script,
partial root, actual exit and invocation journal remain preserved.
[V1 evidence](../metadata/full_weighted_timing_software_failure_20261004_v1.json).
V2 reaches the first eligible cohort and uses content-addressed review arrays.
All three native timing grids passed, then a separate custody fixture failed
because its parent directory was absent.
[V2 evidence](../metadata/full_weighted_timing_software_failure_20261004_v2.json).
Fresh V3 creates that fixture parent. The V2 producer, reader, numeric modules,
custody helper, complete grid, tolerances and caps are unchanged.

V3's actual original tool wait exited zero. Its exact wrapper PID/create/command,
two original wrapper journal entries and manager start/completion were verified;
all 442 transport/source/generated bindings were freshly checked. Native child
CPU use was 130.07 seconds and peak RSS 186,900,480 bytes. These are software
measurements, not full-data timing or memory estimates. Fifteen rehashed
output/checkpoint alterations, fifteen malformed numerical exports and twelve
compact source-closure alterations were rejected, with restored positive bytes.

## Production resources and execution gate

The full timing stage requires actual closed four-control numerical producer,
independent latent readback and original two-journal closure. Its original
numerical closer is the sole upstream scheduling dependency. Timing producer,
reader and closer are separate identity-bound jobs; neither queues model fits.

Prospective workers use two CPUs, 32 GiB, no swap, one BLAS thread and 24-GiB
native address space. Per-stage CPU allocation is 604,800 seconds and per-file
limit 2 GiB; CPU allocation is a capacity cap, not an ETA. Planning accounts for
every original cohort row, 30 designs, 60 response arrays, four D arrays and
five rank-301 factors in an all-review case, plus census/probe/review records.
The 768-GiB output/scratch allowance and 868-GiB free-disk threshold do not assume
compression or cross-cohort sharing. Allowances are not enforced directory
quotas or observed full-data peaks. Available disk is checked before launch
and at each cohort.

The [prepared full plan](../metadata/full_weighted_shared_entity_timing_plan_20261004_v2.json)
pins the original unlaunched fitting draft without changing its bytes. The
[resource inventory](../metadata/full_weighted_shared_entity_timing_resources_20261004_v2.json)
budgets 559.39 GiB of raw arrays/records before the scratch margin.
The [three-job timing queue](../metadata/full_weighted_shared_entity_timing_launches_20261004_v2.json)
waits for original numerical closure before execution. Queueing is not completed
real-data timing. The original fitting draft's `full_timing_implemented=false`
is historical; this new independently pinned stage records the implementation.

After real timing closure, enforced fitting resources and an execution plan
remain required, followed by complete real fit readback and calibration.
Weighting acceptance, variance attribution, accepted phylogenetic/reconciliation/
dating framework and all eight biological aims remain unfinished. GPU structure
prediction stays paused and no new charge is incurred.
