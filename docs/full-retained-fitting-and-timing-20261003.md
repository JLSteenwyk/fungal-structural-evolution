The complete fitting producer and independent reader are implemented and have
passed software qualification. Whole-data timing is queued after the current
process covariance reader and retained V3 qualification close successfully.

## Scope and model

The original 75,188 cases, 622,080 fixed settings, 4,340 cohorts, 130,200
designs and 260,400 design/response inputs remain explicit. Both responses,
ML/REML, signed/unsigned incidence and five working trees yield **5,208,000
potential candidates and 12,441,600 setting links**. Exact input reuse changes
repeated computation, not the biological sampling or effective sample size.
Every constant, insufficient, dependent or numerical-review case stays in the
census and setting export.

The source adapter requires separately closed original seven-kernel
qualification, exact integer covariance-cone certificates and retained V3
qualification. It reconstructs original design/response identities and binds
each candidate to its original audit, retained audit and exact certificate.
Of 8,680 closed cohort/mode certificates, 7,232 have four retained components
and 1,448 have five. The exception basis keeps the model-pair term. These are
operator proofs, not accepted designs or fitted effects.

Numerical candidates use the already qualified retained bridge. Inherited
principal Gram matrices and error envelopes remain unchanged. The additional
fresh incidence guard and independent latent guard must also pass. The same
bounded production multistart optimizer and independent component-spectral
replays, curvature checks and three searches remain required. Agreement does
not prove a global optimum. The production draft preserves the original
gradient tolerances 1e-6 and column batch 32; the synthetic numerical cases
used gradient tolerances 3e-6 and smaller batches. A stricter production
failure remains a review, without loosening tolerances.

## Full-data timing and resources

All 5.208 million identities are censused. A representative is measured for
every eligible cohort/mode/tree/method/outcome group, at most 173,600 groups.
The two declared variance points compare production and independent
likelihood/score values, coefficients, conditional covariance, scale and
variance components. Source validation, constructors, production qualification
and independent latent qualification are measured separately.

Budget-weighted planning includes both guards used by production fitting,
three starts with 500 search evaluations plus final replay, independent
curvature and three independent searches. Primary allocation is 1,503
evaluations per candidate; independent allocation is 1,522 for q4 and 1,526
for q5. Conditional extrapolation uses the larger observed evaluation cost
at the two measured points. It does not mathematically bound unseen optimizer
points, convergence or wall time. Source loading, export and unmeasured
review groups are not silently included in an ETA.

The timing reader reconstructs the entire original census, source identities,
selection, exported agreement fields and planning sums. It reproduces all
claimed construction/precision-review groups. It explicitly does **not**
independently reimplement hardware timing or replay successful timing probes;
full fitting has its own independent numerical reader. Full source/artifact
hashes and both original producer/reader completion journals are required for
timing accounting closure.

Timing producer and reader each use two CPUs, 32 GiB, no swap and one BLAS
thread, with native 24-GiB address space, 604,800 CPU-second and 2-GiB-per-file
limits. The CPU allocation is 168 core-hours per stage, not a finish estimate.
The output allowance is 64 GiB with a minimum 164 GiB free disk; it is planning
and monitored free space, not a total directory quota. Preparation observed
10,321 GiB free disk and 148.8 GiB available RAM. Whole-data runtime remains
uncalibrated; source loading and actual group timing must precede a fitting
launch decision.

Prospective fitting records allow 64 KiB per candidate, 32 KiB per independent
audit and 1 KiB per link: 488.67 GiB uncompressed before other scratch. Reserve
1 TiB output/scratch and maintain 1,124 GiB free before fitting. Array ceilings
and these allowances are not measured process peaks. The proposed fitting
worker uses two CPUs/32 GiB/no swap; no such worker is running yet. Costs use
existing authorized local CPU resources with no new charge. GPU inference
stays paused.
