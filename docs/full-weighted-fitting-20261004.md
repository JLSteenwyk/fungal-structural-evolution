# Complete four-control fitting exports and independent reader

The full fitting producer, original-setting exports and independent reader are
implemented and software qualified. An immutable full-scope draft preserves
20,832,000 potential candidates and 49,766,400 setting links. Actual weighted
numerical closure, whole-grid timing and resource enforcement still gate real
fitting. No real fit worker has been launched or queued.

## Full original scope and numerical checks

All 75,188 original cases, 4,340 cohorts, 130,200 designs, 260,400 response
inputs and 622,080 settings remain required, with four controls, both loading
modes, five trees, both outcomes and ML/REML. Original source exclusions,
constant responses, covariance dependencies and all numerical reviews remain
in the grid. Identical node/pair diagonals retain separate policy identities.

The [qualified source adapter and actual-D candidate bridge](weighted-shared-entity-candidates-20261004.md)
require closed source reconstruction and weighted numerical qualification.
Each fit retains its actual reciprocal-control D, exact basis certificate,
closed covariance audit, design/response hashes, policy, mode, tree and method.
Both the production and independent spectral likelihoods receive that same D.
The independent reader adds the separate latent guard, deterministic start
replay, curvature and independent searches. Numerical agreement does not prove
a global optimum, weighting acceptance or inferential calibration.

## Checkpoints, links and independent replay

`prepare_full_weighted_shared_entity_fits.py` writes 4,800 candidate rows per
original cohort, its original-setting link segment and a completion checkpoint.
It records every failed fit and non-ready row, without reducing the source
grid. Completed chunks can be reused only with unchanged stage contract,
source identity, complete row order, hashes, statuses and link reconstruction.
Completed producers refuse restart. Incomplete or failed files are preserved;
the workflow does not automatically restart failed scientific jobs.

`readback_full_weighted_shared_entity_fits.py` reconstructs the complete input
grid and replays every candidate through the frozen independent numeric reader.
For each original setting row it checks the original zero-based ordinal, digest,
all string-valued fields and every policy/mode/tree/method link. Duplicate
source rows remain distinct. Independent candidate and link statuses are
exported separately; no candidate or link becomes scientifically eligible.

The reader binds its checkpoints to the original producer receipt and plan.
On checkpoint reuse it recomputes every numerical result and checks exact
saved records and independently reconstructed links. This costs a numerical
replay and preserves byte-identical completed exports. A newly hashed local
checkpoint cannot promote a source review. Completed readers refuse alternate
output names. Missing candidates, changed outputs and mismatched numerical
results abort readback and preserve partial files.

Numerical rejection captures include expected identity, the exact saved
candidate representation, exception, original case rows, D, X, response and
species factor. Closed original operator/source bindings remain attached.
Segmentation bounds individual files; it is not biological subsampling.

150 designs, 300 responses and 600 settings: 24,000 candidate rows and
48,000 setting links per grid. Three complete grids total 72,000 rows and
144,000 links, including common/exceptional pair bases, qualified nonuniform
inputs and all original source reviews/constants.

Full-grid native fit and native-reader branches are explicitly mocked to test
accounting, failure preservation and checkpoints. Parent source closures and
original-journal fixtures are synthetic. A separate test serializes and
independently replays all 64 saved actual numerical fits through the frozen
actual-D spectral/start/curvature/search reader. No full-grid mock is presented
as a real fit or as independent numerical inference.

Seventeen rehashed output/checkpoint alterations and twelve compact source
closure alterations were rejected. Seven exact numerical failure captures
include original arrays. Producer and reader interruption tests retain two
completed chunks; restored continuation preserves their bytes. Rehashed reader
checkpoint promotion is rejected by fresh numerical replay. Completed
producer/reader restarts are refused, and positive source bindings are restored
and freshly checked.

## Unlaunched production draft and remaining gates

The [full fitting draft](../metadata/full_weighted_shared_entity_fit_draft_plan_20261004_v1.json)
keeps the existing production gradient tolerances 1e-6, independent column
batch 32, variance cap 1e6 and three starts with 500 search evaluations each.
The separate software numerical cases use the existing synthetic tolerance
3e-6 and batch 3. Passing those software checks does not qualify a real
production candidate under stricter settings; all actual failures remain reviews.

The prospective [resource inventory](../metadata/full_weighted_shared_entity_fit_resource_inventory_20261004_v1.json)
allows 1,954.69 GiB uncompressed exports, a proposed 3-TiB output/scratch reserve
and a 3,172-GiB free-disk threshold. Proposed workers use two CPUs/32 GiB,
one BLAS thread, no swap and 24-GiB address space. These fitting resources are
not installed. They are allowances, not measured bounds or directory quotas.

Whole-grid timing must retain all potential candidate identities and every
eligible cohort/policy/mode/tree/outcome/method group, up to 694,400 groups.
Both variance points, constructor/qualification costs and optimizer/audit budgets
must be accounted for; unmeasured reviews cannot silently enter an ETA. Runtime
remains uncalibrated. A complete timing implementation, actual full numerical
closure, real timing and installed resources remain required before any fit
launch, followed by full independent real fit closure and inferential calibration.
Existing local CPU resources imply no new charge; GPU prediction stays paused.
All eight biological aims and the full original project remain incomplete.
