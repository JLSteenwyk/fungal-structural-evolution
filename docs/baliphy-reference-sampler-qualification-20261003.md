retains two failed selected chains and allocation warnings in other attempts.
The [reference initializer](baliphy-reference-initialization-20261003.md)

representation. Startup fidelity alone does not show how proposals behave
after initialization. The short grid checks early alignment expansion,
allocation outcomes, scalar logs and saved native outputs across every input.
It does not prove that the old failures are repaired or that longer sampling

## Admission and resources

The [immutable plan](../metadata/baliphy_reference_sampler_qualification_plan_20261003.json)
binds 991 source/input/configuration hashes. The producer requires successful
closure of **all 1,620 corrected startups**, zero unsuccessful startups and
405 complete quartets. It checks the prerequisite archive, every bound source
and every startup identity, fresh seed, original seed, prior, role and alias.
Incomplete startup results prevent sampling; they never select a smaller grid.
The dependency wrapper also verifies the original closure process identity
and actual invocation-linked terminal journal before starting the producer.

Allocation-risk membership uses **any** warning or allocation failure in the
full initial-plus-selected-recovery census. The 48 GiB cap applies to every
role, prior and input variant in those families, rather than just the two
failed chains. FIFO admission prevents smaller roles from repeatedly
bypassing a waiting larger role. The entire declared address-space amount
remains reserved through native execution and its Python output audit.
Reservations are not measured peak RSS, and address-space caps are not proof
that an attempt will succeed. Actual whole-group memory is separately capped.

An unexpected controller/integrity exception halts new admissions before its
reservation is released, so an exceptional native process group cannot cause
new jobs to oversubscribe memory. Already admitted attempts retain their
limits. Native nonzero exits are retained as explicit outcomes without
automatic retries. Incomplete native attempts or a partial reservation journal
require manual review; they cannot silently restart or adopt orphaned outputs.
No settings for unrelated jobs or GPU prediction were changed.

Historical successful worker time scaled from 1,000 to 20 iterations is
116,072 seconds, or 32.24 worker hours. This excludes the two failed selected
chains and does not calibrate the new initialization, startup overhead,
workspace or output size. The sum of all declared wall limits is 458.75 worker
hours; it is a limit inventory, not a finish ETA. At preparation, available
RAM was 450.84 GiB and disk space 10,379.24 GiB. Availability is checked again
before launch and before producer execution. The output allowance is a
planning estimate; per-file limits and the free-disk guard are enforced.

## Output checks and limitations

Every attempt preserves its exact configuration, command, process identity,
receipt, stdout/stderr, scalar logs, runtime tree, saved alignments and native
site-property files. The reader checks their hashes and reconstructs every
serialized role disposition from the original native receipt.

For a successful role, all 21 scalar rows and all three saved alignment frames
(iterations 0, 10 and 20) must be present. Checks cover runtime/source rooted
clades, nonroot branch lengths, every node label and alignment dimension,
observed tip residue preservation, state alphabet, finite score arithmetic,
and all four ancestral candidate mappings in every saved frame. A fully
successful grid would contain 4,860 saved alignments and 19,440 candidate
frames. These figures describe computational integrity, not adequate sample
size. Root, tree, model and biological interpretation remain conditional.

Failed header-only or malformed partial scalar logs remain linked to their
native files with an explicit decoder issue; they cannot erase the failure
or stop accounting for the other roles. Nonfinite scalar tokens, allocation
warnings and nonpositive parameter review events remain visible. Positive
infinite gamma shape is the explicitly supported native equal-rate limit;
other nonfinite tokens remain distinct. The nonpositive screen is not a
complete prior-support proof, including the shifted mean-length boundary.

The saved native site-property files are retained and hashed. This stage does
not independently replay every joint site-property array, and it does not
compute adequate posterior diagnostics from 20 iterations. The producer and
serialized reader share the output decoder; the reader is not a third
independent likelihood implementation. Final computational closure requires
the complete source/artifact archive and both original completion journals.

These roles reuse the fresh startup seeds, whose earlier executions were
startup-only. Once short MCMC starts, the seeds have been used for sampling.
Future independent longer chains require a new namespace disjoint from both
the original chains and this qualification; samples will not be concatenated.
