# Gene and isoform reconciliation

Run `python scripts/download_gene_annotations.py` to acquire assembly-matched NCBI GFFs for the 519 NCBI-backed taxa in the working manifest. The script verifies publisher MD5, records SHA256 and validates feature rows. It resumes by checksum and prevents concurrent writers. Missing or invalid annotations are recorded as errors, never silently interpreted as absence of genes. Five published outgroup bundles supply annotations integrated by `map_proteins_to_genes.py`; the two sanchytrid taxa still require gene-model reconciliation.

Run `python scripts/map_proteins_to_genes.py` to process the completed annotation downloads. This snapshots current progress while acquisition continues. Exact, versioned protein accessions are followed through GFF Parent links to local gene features. Repeated rows of a multipart CDS remain one feature; multi-parent links are retained. Protein mappings to multiple genes and proteins without a resolved gene remain explicit. Gene IDs are scoped to a taxon and an exact annotation checksum, not assumed stable across annotation versions. These conventions follow [NCBI's GFF documentation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/datasets/docs/v2/reference-docs/file-formats/annotation-files/about-ncbi-gff3/).

For the four published GFF bundles, exact protein IDs correspond to mRNA IDs and are followed through Parent links to genes; no protein-ID convention is inferred for unrelated annotations. Creolimax's GTF supplies exact transcript and gene identifiers on CDS rows. These source-specific modes are recorded in each taxon's summary. Their provenance is checked against the downloaded external-file receipts.

For the two sanchytrids, first run `python scripts/verify_sanchytrid_coordinates.py`. Protein headers carry ORF coordinates; the audit extracts the stated interval from the published genome, reverse complements when required, and demands exact translation under table 1 after terminal-stop normalization. A contig coverage-suffix discrepancy is accepted only if the node/length prefix identifies one contig and its translation matches. All coordinates are bounds-checked. The audit verified all 7,220 Amoeboradix proteins and 9,367 of 9,368 Sanchytrium proteins. One Sanchytrium interval extends beyond its deposited contig. Verified CDSs and mapping tables remain under `results/sanchytrid_coordinates/`, with checksums and counts in `metadata/sanchytrid_coordinate_audit.json`.

These records enter mapping as `provisional_orf`, with coordinate-derived ORF identifiers in the shared `gene_ids_json` field. They are explicitly not `unique_gene` assignments: overlapping ORFs, isoforms and annotation/assembly discrepancies still need assessment. Representative selection retains all provisional ORFs independently and flags them as unresolved for gene-copy interpretation. The out-of-bounds protein remains unmapped. Partial-CDS status is `unknown` for external mapping modes where the parser has not established it.

Outputs are per-taxon protein-to-gene tables under `results/gene_mapping/` and a versioned summary `metadata/gene_mapping_snapshot.json`. The summary reports genes with multiple protein products; this is not a count of duplications or alternative splice events. Protein sequences remain intact for structural reuse, and the existing raw-proteome BUSCO results retain their original meaning.

Run `python scripts/select_gene_representatives.py --output results/gene_representatives/full-v1` after mappings are available. The versioned baseline chooses the longest protein within each uniquely mapped gene, breaking equal-length ties lexically by exact protein accession. It retains proteins with unresolved mappings independently and flags them as unsuitable for gene-copy counts. Every alternative product remains in the source proteome with a decision-table entry. Outputs record source, mapping, selection and FASTA checksums. Incomplete taxon snapshots are explicitly marked staging; do not treat them as the full orthology dataset.

Before orthogroup inference, investigate partial CDSs, unmapped accessions and multi-locus proteins. Longest-protein selection is an explicit baseline, not proof of the biologically canonical isoform; evaluate alternative representative policies for leading findings. Reassess apparent BUSCO duplication attributable to isoforms. Never collapse separate loci merely because their sequences are identical.
