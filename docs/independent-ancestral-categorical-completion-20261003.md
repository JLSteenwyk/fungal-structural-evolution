# Complete ancestral categorical comparison

The full independent categorical comparison has completed production,
serialized readback and source/artifact/journal closure for all 405 requested
model quartets. It retains 403 complete quartets and both quartets with a
failed native chain. This establishes diagnostic accounting and ordinary
numerical agreement; it does not qualify any ancestral posterior. All eight
biological aims remain incomplete.

Each complete quartet retains both discard-through-250 and
discard-through-500 results, every declared state, full temporal patterns,
coordinate mappings and multiplicities. Each cutoff represents 22,871,876
coordinates. The same coordinates occur in both cutoffs, and pattern counts
are repeated across model/chain settings; these totals are not independent
biological sample sizes.

No pattern passes the observed-state categorical screen. Absence of observed
variation does not establish posterior certainty; absent/constant states
remain explicit reviews. Numerical reproduction of a diagnostic is separate
from adequate mixing of the sampled ancestral states and alignments.

For ordinary defined comparisons, maximum absolute differences are
8.88e-16 for R-hat, 6.54e-12 for bulk ESS, 6.17e-12 for tail ESS and 7.11e-15
for mean MCSE. The original absolute and relative comparison tolerances
remain 1e-8. All scalar/length and biological posterior gates remain unchanged.

## Explicit unresolved numerical comparisons

Fifteen state indicators encounter exactly zero reachable Geyer
autocorrelation-pair boundaries. Integer/Fraction certificates enumerate the
admissible floating-point truncation outcomes. Both implementations match
those certified alternatives at the original tolerance, but the differing
reported values remain **unresolved numerical reviews**, not agreed values.
Other discrepancies still fail the comparison.

There are two affected indicators at cutoff 250 and thirteen at cutoff 500.
Two indicators have more than one flagged metric, producing seventeen review
table rows. For example, one cutoff-500 lysine indicator in OG0001200 has bulk
ESS values 188.67018722995684 and 199.59370238699853; its coordinate
multiplicity is 28. This is a numerical boundary case, not evidence of
adequate effective sample size or an evolutionary effect.

The original failed v1 comparison and its 37 group checkpoints remain
preserved. The completed v2 did not loosen ordinary tolerances, remove
constant states or select a smaller grid. Its serialized reader shares the
new estimator; it is not a third independent numerical implementation.
